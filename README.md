# 🛡️ ScamSathi AI

**Think Before You Trust.**

ScamSathi AI is your personal digital safety assistant. It helps you find out if a message is a scam before you reply, click a link or send money.

## Our Vision

Every day, people in Pakistan get fake prize messages, fake job offers, phishing links and fraud payment requests. Many lose money because they can't tell what's real.

ScamSathi wants everyone to be able to check a suspicious message in seconds, in a language they understand, with no technical knowledge needed.

## What You Can Do

- **Check a message:** paste any SMS, WhatsApp message, email or job offer.
- **Check a screenshot:** upload a picture of a suspicious message.
- **Spot job & earning scams:** fake internships, task scams, registration fees and work-from-home tricks.

## What You Get

- A clear **risk level** (High, Medium or Low)
- The **warning signs** found in the message
- A **simple explanation** of why it may be a scam
- **What to do next** to stay safe

## Available In

## Your Privacy

No sign-up, no account, and your messages are never saved. Please remove personal details like your CNIC, bank account number or OTP before checking.

## Please Note

ScamSathi gives guidance, not a guarantee. If you're unsure, contact your bank or the official organisation directly.
